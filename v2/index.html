Fabrication & Architectural Capabilities.

Specialist metalworking services for architects, builders, and custom residential commissions across Tamil Nadu.

01

Steel Fabrication

Structural hollow section engineering, custom cold-rolled steel profiling, and high-tolerance TIG/MIG welding for architectural commissions.

02

Custom Steel Doors

Monumental pivot portals, thermal break profiles, acoustic seals, and bespoke framed entrance systems with timber or glass infills.

03

Metal Staircases

Custom central mono-stringers, floating cantilever treads, and straight flight structural stair systems engineered to architectural drawings.

04

Spiral Staircases

Helical curved plate stringers, continuous center-column spiral stairways, and radius-bent balustrades for statement vertical circulation.

05

Interior Steel Work

Minimalist steel-framed room dividers, glass-insert partition walls, bespoke interior railings, and custom architectural metal features.

06

Exterior Steel Work

Heavy-duty perimeter boundary fencing, structural exterior frames, protective compound enclosures, and weather-resistant gates.

07

Architectural Elevation Work

Multi-level building facade metal screening, vertical sun-shading louvers, and custom exterior architectural steel detailing.

08

Canopies

Cantilevered portico awnings, structural terrace pergolas, and engineered steel frameworks built to endure tropical monsoons and wind loads.

09

Automated Gates & Doors

Motorized sliding gates, telescopic dual-leaf systems, and automated swing entrance portals with remote and smart sensor actuation.

10

UPVC Work

Precision UPVC window and door systems offering superior thermal efficiency, acoustic insulation, and weatherproofing for modern residences.

11

Glass Work

Architectural laminated safety glass integration, frameless glass partitions, structural glass floor panels, and custom decorative glasswork.

12

Toughened Glass Work

Heavy-gauge heat-treated safety glass for high-traffic entrances, canopy roofs, cantilevered stair balustrades, and structural facades.

13

Aluminium Work

Architectural aluminium extrusions, composite facade paneling, custom sliding profiles, and lightweight weather-resistant exterior elements.
