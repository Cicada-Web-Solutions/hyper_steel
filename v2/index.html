Fabrication & Architectural Capabilities.

Specialist metalworking disciplines for architects, builders, and custom residential commissions across Tamil Nadu.

01

Steel Fabrication

Heavy structural steelwork, customized trusses, structural frames, and precision CNC metal profiling crafted to millimeter tolerances.

02

Custom Steel Doors

Monumental pivot doors, French steel entryways, and thermal-break metal doors with custom push pulls and acoustic sealing.

03

Metal Staircases

Cantilevered floating treads, sawtooth plate stringers, and structural spine staircases designed as sculptural centerpieces.

04

Spiral Staircases

Continuous center-column spirals and curved helical stairways crafted with seamless rolled pipe handrails and non-slip treads.

05

Interior Steel Work

Bespoke steel-and-glass room dividers, mezzanine platforms, decorative partition screens, and architectural furniture frames.

06

Exterior Steel Work

Weather-resistant perimeter railings, structural pergolas, security grilles, and heavy-duty compound architectural framework.

07

Architectural Elevation Work

Engineered vertical louver fins, bespoke building facades, decorative 3D metal cladding, and monumental architectural arches.

08

Canopies

Cantilevered entrance porticos, rooftop terrace shade structures, and glass-covered steel pergolas built for monsoon endurance.

09

Automated Gates & Doors

Telescopic track systems, cantilever sliding gates, and motorized swing gates with heavy-duty smart automation motors.

10

UPVC Work

Multi-chamber thermal insulated UPVC window and door systems with high sound attenuation, weather seals, and multi-point locks.

11

Glass & Toughened Glass Work

Frameless glass balustrades, spider-fitting curtain walls, and tempered architectural glass integrated with structural steel.

12

Aluminium Work

Precision aluminium slimline facades, powder-coated partition systems, structural extrusions, and architectural louvers.
